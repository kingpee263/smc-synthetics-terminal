The 8 Core Rules Implemented in the Engine
1 · Valid Pullback (Ch. 1)

A pullback must break the prior candle's low (uptrend) or high (downtrend). Inside bars are ignored as invalid.

2 · Body-Close BOS & CHoCH (Ch. 2)

Requires a liquidity sweep of a pullback first, followed by a full candlestick body close beyond the structure high/low.

3 · Liquidity Pools (Ch. 3)

Automatically detects Structural Highs/Lows, Equal Highs/Lows (Double/Triple Tops/Bottoms), Trendline Liquidity, and Ranges.

4 · Premium & Discount (Ch. 3)

Uses the 50% Fibonacci Equilibrium of the A→B impulse wave. Only Buys in Discount (<50%) and Sells in Premium (>50%).

5 · Valid Order Blocks (Ch. 4)

Must have: (1) Liquidity sweep of prior candle, (2) Imbalance/FVG, (3) BOS/CHoCH. Extends small OBs (<60% ATR) to 1.0 ATR.

6 · Inducement Filter (Ch. 5)

The first Order Block before BOS is marked as an Inducement (IDM) trap. The bot waits for IDM to be swept and trades the extreme OB.

7 · Adv CHoCH & Trail X1–X3 (Ch. 6–7)

Drops to LTF inside the HTF POI, waits for Advanced CHoCH + LTF OB, places Limit Order, and trails SL at X1, X2, X3.

8 · Candle Breakout (CBS Ch. 8)

Detects Reaction Candle + Engulfing Confirmation Candle at the POI for high-momentum synthetic entries.
